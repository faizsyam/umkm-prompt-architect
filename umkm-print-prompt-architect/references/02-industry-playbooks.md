# 02. Industry Playbooks

**Persona: Brand Consultant with sector knowledge.** You know what owners in the sector mean, what customers decide on in 2 seconds, and what every competitor already looks like.

**Principle.** A category tells you what customers *expect*; the USP tells you why *this* business. A good design fulfils the category's **recognition cues** (recognized in 2 seconds) and adds one **ownable twist** (remembered). Rows below are **starting hypotheses to verify**, not conclusions: use them to (a) sharpen interview probes, (b) spot likely USP territories (`01` §2), (c) seed hero and option generation in `04`, (d) know the **clichés to avoid** (feed Exclusions in `14` §7). Blend rows for hybrids; ignore them when the owner's story points elsewhere.

**Columns:** *Recognition cues* (must read instantly) · *Probe → USP territories* · *Hero/subject seeds* · *Must-have content* · *Avoid (generic defaults)*.

## D. Mixed and unusual
Break the business into **what is sold, how, to whom, what is memorable**; borrow recognition cues from the closest rows **and** derive from its own physical world (counter, tools, packaging, ritual, route, hands at work). Unique businesses deserve unique cues. Use "rows from two types" for hybrids (kafe + laundry, toko + kos).

## E. Environment fit: belong, stand out, or both
**Principle:** the sign competes with its surroundings. Decide a stance, state the mechanism, verify with the owner.
| Setting | Typical conditions | Design implication |
|---|---|---|
| Pasar tradisional | dense, colorful clutter, close viewing | win by clarity and one strong color block; large simple mark; avoid adding clutter |
| Ruko / pinggir jalan | motor/mobil speed, long distance | silhouette-readable mark, huge scale contrast, ≤3 text levels |
| Mall / food court | controlled light, brand-rich neighbors | refined finish, stronger concept, shorter text; avoid chain look-alikes |
| Kampus / sekolah | young, social-first, fast scanning | contemporary cues, shareable look, IG handle prominent |
| Perumahan / gang | slow walkers, local familiarity | warm, neighbor-scale, phone/WA prominent |
| Kantor / pusat bisnis | lunch rush, time pressure | speed, clarity, menu glanceable |
| Wisata / terminal / bandara | strangers, multilingual, quick decision | iconic hero, minimal text, price clarity |
| Online-first (GoFood/Instagram) | thumbnail size, packaging as media | strong silhouette at small size, consistent packaging identity |
| Night trade | lamps, backlight | high ground contrast, luminous accents, backlit-safe colors |
**Stance options:** *Belong* (shared category cues, then one twist) · *Stand out* (contrast in value, color, shape or scale while keeping recognition) · *Hybrid* (belong in structure, stand out in one device). **Check** sun glare (outdoor), night lighting, neighbor clutter, traffic speed, distance, and **look-alike risk** with big brands' color-and-shape combinations.
