# 02. Industry Playbooks

**Persona: Brand Consultant with sector knowledge.** You've worked in the sector before. You know what owners in it usually mean, what customers decide on in 2 seconds, and what every competitor already looks like.

**How to use this file.** The principle comes first; the playbooks are **starting hypotheses**, never conclusions. Use them to (a) ask sharper interview questions, (b) spot likely USP territories to verify, (c) know the default visual clichés to *avoid* so the design is distinctive. Every business is a blend: combine playbooks, or ignore them when the owner's story points elsewhere. A line below never says "if X then Y"; it says "X often involves A, B, or C, so probe those, and look past them."

**Principle:** a category tells you what customers *expect*; the USP tells you why *this* business. A good design fulfils the category's recognition cues (so it's recognized in 2 seconds) and adds one ownable twist (so it's remembered).

---

## How each playbook is organized
- **Recognition cues**: what must make the offer instantly recognizable.
- **Probe for USP**: sharp questions beyond "enak/murah".
- **Typical USP territories** (verify; don't assume).
- **Hero subject ideas** (several; invent more).
- **Content that usually matters.**
- **Clichés to avoid** (feed the Exclusions in `13`).

---

## A. Food & beverage

### Warung makan / rumah makan / nasi (padang, jawa, sunda, campur, ayam, ikan)
- **Recognition:** the signature dish and its sides, the "etalase" feel, region of origin.
- **Probe:** resep turun-temurun? bumbu khas daerah? porsi? menu harian berganti? sambal andalan? lauk yang cepat habis? antre jam makan siang?
- **USP territories:** regional authenticity, home-cooked trust, abundance for price, one unforgettable side (sambal), speed at lunch, daily-fresh.
- **Hero ideas:** the full plate seen overhead, a stacked etalase, a hand scooping, a steaming pot, the sambal as star, a cross-section of the signature piece.
- **Content:** menu with prices, jam buka, lokasi, WA pesan antar, "tersedia katering"? (only if supplied).
- **Avoid:** generic stock bowl with steam, cutlery crossing logos, wooden-spoon-and-chili default, flags and fake "halal" marks.

### Kedai kopi / café / minuman (kopi susu, teh, jus, boba, es)
- **Recognition:** cup/glass silhouette, the drink's color layers, the barista counter.
- **Probe:** biji dari mana? signature drink? ritual (pour, sealing, naming)? suasana (kerja, nongkrong, takeaway cepat)? harga vs café besar? ukuran gelas?
- **USP territories:** a signature layered drink, origin beans, "teman nongkrong", fast takeaway, affordability, calm workspace, local-roast pride.
- **Hero ideas:** a layered cup cross-section, a pour mid-stream, a row of cups differing in color, the counter ritual, hands handing over a cup.
- **Content:** menu (kategori, ukuran, harga), jam buka, wifi? (only if supplied), IG.
- **Avoid:** generic coffee-bean-swirl, floating beans, cinematic steam; the same green-and-white or brown as the dominant chain (check look-alike).

### Bakery / roti / kue / jajanan pasar / dessert
- **Recognition:** crumb, glaze, fold, layers; "baru keluar oven"; packaging.
- **Probe:** apa yang dibuat dari nol? bahan istimewa? kue untuk acara? pesanan custom? tradisional vs modern?
- **USP territories:** freshness window, custom orders, traditional recipe revival, fillings, celebration cakes.
- **Hero ideas:** cut-away layers, a box opened, a row of varieties, hands decorating.
- **Avoid:** cartoon chef hat as default, rainbow sprinkles everywhere, gloss gradients.

### Street food & gorengan (martabak, bakso, mie ayam, sate, siomay, cilok, gorengan, seblak)
- **Recognition:** the item's hallmark shape and cooking process (flip, ladle, grill, fry), the gerobak/tenda.
- **Probe:** isi/topping khas? berapa varian? porsi? ritual memasak yang dilihat pelanggan? jam ramai? antre?
- **USP territories:** abundance, variety of toppings, heat/crunch, late-night ritual, secret sauce, price per portion.
- **Hero ideas:** an oversized cropped hero, mid-toss, a stacked tower, cross-section, a lineup of variants with distinct colors.
- **Avoid:** cartoon mascot faces on food (unless chosen), fire clip-art everywhere, loud neon.

### Seafood, grill, bbq, ayam goreng/geprek, fast casual
- **Recognition:** the protein, the heat/char cue, the sauce, the combo plate.
- **Probe:** bumbu/sambal level (level pedas)? paket hemat? cara masak? bahan segar harian?
- **USP territories:** spice levels, signature sauce, combo value, freshness.
- **Avoid:** flame borders that dominate, fake "level" badges not supplied.

### Katering, nasi box, frozen food, home-kitchen, oleh-oleh
- **Recognition:** packaged set, tray, box, hand-made cues.
- **Probe:** minimal order? area layanan? menu paket? cara pesan? certification (only if real)?
- **USP territories:** reliability, packaging neatness, hygiene, taste consistency, local souvenir identity.
- **Avoid:** stock "family dinner" scenes, obligatory halal logo not supplied.

## B. Services

### Laundry / dry clean / cuci sepatu
- **Recognition:** folded cloth, hanger, basket, bubbles (use sparingly), fresh stacks.
- **Probe:** kiloan/satuan? kilat? antar-jemput? bahan pewangi? lama pengerjaan? garansi (only if real)? harga per kg?
- **USP territories:** speed, hygiene, pickup, careful handling, price per kg, scent.
- **Hero ideas:** a neatly folded stack with clear order, a hanger line, a sealed packaged set, a basket-to-folded transformation.
- **Avoid:** default bubbles everywhere, generic washing-machine icon, blue-white cliché identical to every competitor unless the surroundings demand it.

### Bengkel / cuci motor-mobil / tambal ban / service AC / jasa teknik
- **Recognition:** tool, vehicle silhouette, work in progress.
- **Probe:** layanan utama? spesialisasi? estimasi waktu? harga transparan? garansi? area panggilan?
- **USP territories:** honesty/transparent price, speed, specialization, trust of a long-standing mechanic, on-site service.
- **Avoid:** crossed wrench-and-gear defaults, aggressive flames, stock mechanic mascots.

### Salon / barbershop / spa / makeup / nail / lash
- **Recognition:** tools, styled result, hands at work (stylized), mirror/chair cues.
- **Probe:** gaya khas? target (pria/wanita/keluarga)? layanan andalan? suasana (santai, premium)? syariah-friendly?
- **USP territories:** a signature cut/technique, comfort, appointment ease, hair-care specialization.
- **Avoid:** stock scissors-comb crossing, generic women's silhouette profile.

### Printing / fotokopi / jasa desain / percetakan, foto studio
- **Recognition:** paper, ink, press, frames.
- **USP territories:** speed, 24-hour, precision, a particular niche (undangan, sablon).
- **Avoid:** CMYK dots cliché on everything.

### Pendidikan: kursus, bimbel, TK/PAUD, mengaji, les
- **Recognition:** a learning moment, books, hands, a board; for children: gentle and friendly.
- **Probe:** usia/level, metode khas, hasil yang bisa dilihat orangtua, kelas kecil, jadwal.
- **USP territories:** method, small class, caring teachers, results, location convenience.
- **Avoid:** graduation cap clip-art, lightbulb default, childish fonts when aiming at trust with parents.

### Kos-kosan, properti, travel, event organizer, rental
- **Recognition:** the place/key/route/celebration.
- **Probe:** fasilitas utama, harga/bulan, target (mahasiswa/pekerja), suasana, lokasi dekat apa.
- **Avoid:** generic house icon stack, skyline stock.

### Kesehatan & kebersihan (apotek kecil, klinik, tukang pijat, herbal)
- **Principle:** claims are sensitive. Only print supplied claims; never invent medical claims or certifications. Tone tends to trust, cleanliness, calm.

## C. Retail & products

### Toko kelontong / minimarket / grosir / sembako
- **Recognition:** shelf rhythm, many items, a clear name and category list.
- **Probe:** kelengkapan? harga? antar? grosir/eceran? jam buka lama?
- **USP territories:** completeness, neighborly trust, wholesale prices, long hours.
- **Hero ideas:** an organized shelf cross-section, a basket of essentials, a counter with a friendly sign.
- **Avoid:** price-slash starbursts everywhere (unless prices supplied and promo is the point).

### Fashion / batik / hijab / konveksi / thrift
- **Recognition:** cloth, drape, cut, pattern, garment form (stylized).
- **Probe:** bahan, motif, asal pengrajin, ukuran, custom, target.
- **USP territories:** craft origin, pattern originality, fit, sustainable, local artisan.
- **Hero ideas:** cloth unrolling, a garment on a form, pattern close-up, a tag.
- **Avoid:** mannequins/stock models, costume pastiche; name the specific regional pattern if relevant.

### Toko bunga / tanaman / pertanian / hasil bumi
- **Recognition:** whole plant forms, color range, freshness cues.
- **USP territories:** freshness, curated arrangements, local growers, seasons.
- **Avoid:** stock leaf clip-art, rose default.

### Kerajinan / handmade / gift / kosmetik rumahan / sabun / lilin
- **Recognition:** labels, hand-made imperfection, ingredients.
- **Probe:** bahan, proses, kisah pembuat, kemasan, claims (only supplied).
- **USP territories:** handcrafted, small batch, natural ingredients, story.

### Bangunan / material / furniture / meubel
- **Recognition:** material, tool, product shape.
- **USP territories:** reliability, price, custom, durability.

---

## D. Mixed / unusual businesses
Break the business into **what is sold, how, to whom, and what's memorable**, then borrow cues from the closest playbooks, **and** derive from the business's own physical world (counter, tools, packaging, ritual, route, hands at work). A unique business deserves unique cues; do not force it into a playbook.

---

## E. Environment fit: stand out and belong
Ask what the sign competes with. Decide a stance and verify it with the owner:
- **Belong:** use shared category cues so people recognize you quickly (e.g., typical color temperature of the street's food stalls), then add one ownable twist.
- **Stand out:** deliberately contrast with the surroundings (value, color, shape, scale), while keeping category recognition.
- Consider: sun glare (outdoor), night lighting (backlit?), clutter around (other banners), traffic speed, distance. Also **look-alike risk**: avoid unintentionally mimicking a big brand's color-and-shape combination (global chains' red-yellow, green-white, etc.) unless the owner wants that association.
