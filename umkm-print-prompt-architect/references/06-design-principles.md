# 06. Design Principles

**Persona: Senior Art Director & Design Educator.** You explain why a layout works and diagnose why one fails. You apply principles to *decide*, not to decorate. Each principle becomes a **prompt-ready decision**: numbers for control, mechanisms not adjectives.

**Principle.** Principles are lenses, not checkboxes. Use those that matter for the Design Idea and the medium; when two pull apart (unity vs variety, contrast vs calm), decide by the Design Idea and register. Principles supply the *reasoning*; libraries (`07`-`12`) supply *examples*.

---

## 1. Core principles
| Principle | What it means | Tools / options | Prompt translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Focal point** | one element clearly dominant | size, value, saturation, position, isolation, framing, direction | "the mark is the largest element ≈35% of width; the hero ranks second" |
| **Hierarchy** | ranked importance; each level has a distinct role | scale, weight, color, case, position, spacing, enclosure | ranking list 1…n; focal:supporting ≈ 1.4-2× |
| **Contrast** | difference creates legibility and energy | value (most important), scale, hue, saturation, texture, shape, density, orientation | legibility clause; "≥7:1 mark, ≥4.5:1 info" |
| **Balance** | equilibrium of visual weight | symmetric · asymmetric · radial · mosaic; weight = size × darkness × saturation × complexity × position | "offset by a lighter but larger counterweight" |
| **Proximity / grouping** | related = near; unrelated = apart | spacing, enclosure, alignment, similarity, continuity, closure | "contacts form one aligned cluster" |
| **Alignment** | every element tied to an axis/baseline/edge | shared axis, flush-left/right, center, baseline grid; deliberate breaks | "shared baseline; one deliberate tilt on the mark" |
| **Repetition / rhythm** | repeated treatments build "one hand" | even, staggered, graduated, alternating; one deliberate break | "equal spacing, varied color identity; one oversized exception" |
| **Unity** | parts feel like one piece | one ground, one technique, one type system, one DNA, a connector | "one integrated system, not N panels" |
| **Variety** | enough difference to hold interest | scale, color identity, a surprise element | "one surprise, rest consistent" |
| **White space** | active breathing room | clear space around mark/info; margins; leading | "clear space ≈ half cap height around the mark" |
| **Proportion / scale** | repeatable ratios; size for distance | thirds, 60/30/10, golden-ish 1:1.6, root rectangles, modular scale | "zones ≈ 55/30/15; text height by distance" |
| **Emphasis / economy** | one emphasis per level; remove what doesn't serve | cut test: needed? serves idea? shares DNA? | "no ornament touching text" |
| **Figure/ground** | silhouette separates from field | tonal step, stage shape, outline, color difference | "hero reads as a silhouette on a lighter stage" |
| **Depth (flat)** | depth without 3D | overlap, scale, tonal steps, hard shadow, cut-paper offset | "hard offset shadows only; no gradients" |
| **Direction / flow** | the eye path | diagonals, arrows, gaze, taper, leading lines, connector | "connector leads from hero to contact strip" |
| **Tension / movement** | energy from imbalance | tilt, off-center, crop, overlap, scale jump | "mark tilted 3-5° as a deliberate move" |
| **Framing** | enclosure focuses attention | arches, plates, windows, cartouches | "hero in an arch-shaped window" |
| **Odd-number grouping** | 3, 5 groups look natural | clusters of 3/5, asymmetry | "three featured items, center largest" |
| **Simplicity / restraint** | fewer, stronger moves | one signature move, limited palette | "one signature move echoed once" |
| **Distinctiveness / memory** | stands out in category | swap test, signature move, silhouette test, von Restorff isolation | "one ownable device" |

## 2. Contrast types (decide which carries the hierarchy)
| Type | Effect | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| Value | legibility at distance | primary legibility; check in grayscale |
| Scale | hierarchy, energy | avoid mid-sized everything |
| Hue / temperature | emphasis, mood | vibrating pairs (red/green, saturated blue/red) on thin type |
| Saturation | focus vs recede | all-saturated = noise |
| Texture | interest | busy texture behind text |
| Shape (round/angular) | tension | needs DNA consistency |
| Density (full/empty) | rhythm | |
| Weight (thin/heavy) | type hierarchy | avoid hairlines on flexi |

## 3. Gestalt and perception toolkit
Proximity · similarity · enclosure · continuity · closure · figure/ground · common fate (shared direction) · symmetry · focal isolation (von Restorff) · simplicity (Prägnanz). Use to group info, imply shapes, and keep reading smooth.

## 4. Reading patterns and cognition
| Pattern | Best for | Place along it |
|---|---|---|
| Z-pattern | wide banners | mark top-left → hook across → action bottom-right |
| F-pattern | text-heavy menus, flyers | headers left, key items on the stem |
| Center-out | radial/roundel | mark center, supports around |
| Top-down | X-banner, poster | mark → hero → points → contact |
| Left-to-right | spanduk, strip panels | mark → hero → contact |
| Diagonal | dynamic posters | focal on one diagonal end, action on the other |
| Gutenberg diagram | balanced layouts | primary top-left, terminal bottom-right |
**3-second test:** who you are, what you offer, the hook. **Recognition over recall:** use the category's cues. **Hick's law:** fewer choices on signs; group menu items. **Chunking:** 3-5 items per cluster.

## 5. Proportion and measure systems
Rule of thirds · 60/30/10 (dominant/secondary/accent) · golden-ish 1:1.6 · root-2 / A-series ratios · modular scale for type (1.25, 1.333, 1.5) · 4/8-unit spacing rhythm · margin ≈ 4-6% of the short side · gutters ≈ 1-2× cap height · line-height 1.1-1.2 display, 1.3-1.5 info · optical sizing (round shapes overshoot, centered elements sit slightly above geometric center).

## 6. Composition techniques library
Leading lines · diagonals · overlap/layering · cropping hero · scale jump · negative-space cut · frame-in-frame · grid with one broken cell · symmetry with one asymmetric accent · stack · radial burst · horizon band · tiling/repeat · rule of odds · visual triangles (hero + two supports) · whitespace island for the focal point · cross-zone spillage.

## 7. Cultural, semiotic, accessibility
- Colors, patterns and symbols carry regional/religious/political meanings; use motifs with understanding; no costume pastiche; check unintended party-color combos and big-brand look-alikes; religious cues only if owner-supplied.
- Color-blind safe (don't rely on red/green alone); sufficient size; solid or near-solid plates behind info; avoid busy backgrounds behind small text.

## 8. Print and production awareness
Material (flexi glare/grommets, paper finish, sticker cut line, fabric weave, glass mirror) · process (digital, offset, sablon, DTF) · color (avoid neon; saturated blue-violets/cyans shift in CMYK; very dark flat blacks band on flexi; thin light-on-dark text bleeds) · edges (safe area, full bleed, no crop marks) · resolution intent (large-format tolerates less detail; small formats need simple shapes). See `03` §3.

## 9. Anti-patterns (what makes UMKM prints look generic)
Too many fonts/colors · everything the same size · everything centered · low-contrast text on busy images · mixed icon styles · ornaments touching text · borders on everything · collage of unrelated pictures · all info at one level · text over complex areas · label words instead of icons · floating cliché ingredients · inconsistent price columns · decorative fonts for information · low-res/pixelated logo placement · full-bleed photos with no plate for text · too many starbursts/promos · unfinished edges.

## 10. Principles → decisions (map)
| Need | Lead with |
|---|---|
| Seen from far | focal point, value contrast, scale, silhouette, economy |
| Information-heavy | hierarchy, grouping, grid, repetition, white space, chunking |
| Premium | white space, restraint, proportion, fine contrast, single accent |
| Energetic / street | scale contrast, asymmetry, diagonal, texture, overlap, color blocking |
| Heritage | pattern, proportion, material cues, restraint, cultural accuracy |
| Memorable | swap test, signature move, outside-the-list idea, figure/ground |
| Small formats | economy, silhouette, ≤3 text levels, flat colors |
| Multi-panel | continuity, rhythm, panel hierarchy, fold safety |
