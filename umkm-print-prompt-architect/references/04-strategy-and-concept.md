# 04. Strategy and Concept

**Persona: Creative Director (strategy hat).** You convert a confirmed Discovery Brief into a single, ownable visual idea. You choose by evidence from the interview, not by taste or habit. You know that distinctiveness comes from specificity: the more you know about what is true only of this business, the more precise and unrepeatable the design.

**Input:** the confirmed Discovery Brief + Assumption Ledger. Use only ✔ Confirmed facts for printed content. Use ? Assumed items cautiously (they should have been verified at Gate A) and ✚ Suggested items only if approved.
**Output:** Concept Sheet part 1 = Positioning, Design Idea, USP→device map, decoded style, register dials, Style DNA. (Parts 2: `05`-`11`. Audit: `12`.)

---

## 1. Positioning (one line each)
- **Who** buys.
- **Where and from how far** each piece is seen (per medium).
- **What** makes the business different (the confirmed USP).
- **What it must feel like** in the first 2 seconds.
- **What a viewer must instantly recognize** it as.
- **Environment stance:** belong, or stand out, or both (see `02` §E), and **how** (value, color, shape, scale).

Then write **one Design Idea**: a single visual proposition fusing the differentiator and the personality. It must pass the **swap test** (replace the business and the idea stops making sense). Everything else serves it.

### Ways to find the idea (use several, not one)
- the business's own **physical world** (counter, tools, packaging, ritual, route, hands at work);
- the **customer's moment** (hunger, hurry, celebration, routine);
- the differentiator **taken literally or exaggerated**;
- the personality word as a **metaphor**;
- a **region/craft/tradition** meeting the product;
- a **scale or perspective shift** (macro hero, cutaway, overhead, stacked);
- an **inversion** of the category's habit (what every competitor does → do the opposite, if it fits);
- the **owner's story** (origin, family recipe, a recurring phrase customers say);
- a **metaphor from the environment** (the street, the market, the neighborhood).
Write 3 candidate ideas; reject any that a competitor could use; keep the one with the clearest visual form.

---

## 2. Brand personality (optional lens when the owner gives only feelings)
Archetypes are **a thinking tool, not a label to print**; mix freely, invent others: *the Caregiver (hangat, family, trust)*, *the Everyman (merakyat, neighborly, honest)*, *the Jester (jenaka, playful, cheeky)*, *the Explorer (adventurous flavor)*, *the Creator/Artisan (handmade, craft)*, *the Sage (ahli, careful)*, *the Ruler/Elite (premium, dignified)*, *the Rebel (street, bold)*, *the Lover (indulgence)*, *the Innocent (fresh, simple, clean)*, *the Hero (kuat, cepat, andal)*, *the Magician (surprising, kekinian)*. Translate the chosen personality into **visible cues** (§4), never into words on the sign.

---

## 3. USP and objective mapping
Map every USP/objective to a **visual device** (what the eye sees) and, only when the owner supplied wording, a **text element**. The table shows how, with multiple options each; invent more. **An unmapped USP is a defect.**

| USP / objective | Visual device options |
|---|---|
| Abundance / generosity | overflow past container edges; stacked or avalanching toppings; hero cropped by the frame; items crossing zone boundaries; over-full containers |
| Variety / uniqueness | showcase set with shared structure but distinct color identity; fan, row, or tiered display; menu-board rhythm; one "surprise" item given a highlight device |
| Affordability | clear price element **only if prices were supplied**; otherwise open, uncluttered generosity and an approachable tone; mention in the assumptions line |
| Tradition / experience | craft marks, heritage pattern, hand-lettered detail, year mark (only if given), patina cues |
| Freshness / health | natural ingredients shown whole, airy space, leaf or water cues, clean light ground |
| Speed / convenience | modular order, directional flow, clear grouping, bold simple shapes |
| Trust / hygiene | order, alignment, whitespace, restrained palette, clean lines |
| Premium | fewer elements, finer lines, larger margins, a single controlled accent |
| Community / family | warm gathering cues, people-scale props, friendly curves |
| Craft / handmade | visible hand-made imperfection, tool or process cues, tactile texture, maker's mark |
| Origin / local pride | named regional motif or object, place cue, material of the region |
| Specialization / expertise | a single focused subject, precise detail, a "specialist" sign form |
| Convenience / delivery | route/path cue, package, icon for delivery, clear contact action |
| Late-night / always-on | night palette, lantern/lamp cue, glow from the focal point |
| Surprise / novelty | unexpected scale or pairing, a distinctive shape, a playful twist in the mark |

## 4. Decoding vague style words
Owners give vague intent ("playful, friendly, eye-catching", "classy but affordable", "kekinian", "clean", "mewah", "simple tapi menarik"). Never pass the words through; **decode** them:
1. **Meaning for this business:** who feels it and where it should show (the mark? the food? the layout?).
2. **Visible cues in this category:** what *evidence* conveys it (tilted baselines, chunky shapes, overlap; fine lines and spacing; saturated blocks; soft rounded corners; hand-made imperfection...).
3. **Its limits:** what it must **not** tip into (playful ≠ childish; premium ≠ cold; traditional ≠ dusty; eye-catching ≠ loud and cluttered).
4. **Levers:** convert the cues into decisions across composition, shape language, color, type, illustration, decoration, and scale contrast.
5. **Combine words:** pair them into a single stance (e.g. "playful + premium" = joyful details inside a disciplined structure; "traditional + modern" = heritage materials with contemporary alignment and restraint).

Indonesian words often used: *kekinian* (contemporary, shareable, trend-aware: clean-quirky shapes, bold type, tasteful color blocking, but still the business's idea), *merakyat* (approachable, abundant, familiar), *mewah* (restraint, material richness, fine detail), *ramai/meriah* (festive density, color, overlap), *bersih* (order, whitespace), *adem* (calm, soft tonal, green/earth), *kuat/berani* (high contrast, heavy type), *klasik* (heritage proportion, serif, ornament restrained), *jadul/nostalgia* (era cues: signboard craft, faded paper, enamel signs), *simple tapi menarik* (few elements, one strong move).

## 5. Register dials and Style DNA
Set the dials from the decoded words and the business, then derive everything else from them.

| Dial | Range (continuous; place the design anywhere) |
|---|---|
| **Energy** | calm ... steady ... spirited ... exuberant |
| **Era** | heritage ... timeless ... contemporary ... playful-modern ... retro-revival |
| **Finish** | precise/refined ... hand-crafted ... raw/street-made |
| **Density** | spacious ... balanced ... rich ... abundant |
| **Formality** | dignified ... friendly ... casual ... cheeky |

**How dials translate:** the table gives example translations, not rules; interpolate and blend.

| Register example | Composition | Shape language | Color | Type | Illustration | Decoration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Calm / premium | symmetry, wide margins, few elements | fine, geometric, precise | tonal, muted; light, deep, or dark ground | high-contrast serif, light sans, wide tracking | slim line, minimal fills | sparse; tone-on-tone |
| Warm / nostalgic | grounded, familiar, axial or banded | soft, bracketed, organic | earthy, muted, paper-like | sturdy serif, signwriter or script accent | hand-drawn, slight imperfection | heritage textures; small crafted details |
| Friendly / family | open, balanced | rounded, soft | warm, mid-saturation | rounded serif or friendly sans | simple flat, generous | thematic accents, light |
| Spirited / playful | dynamic: tilts, overlap, scale contrast, crops | chunky, rounded, bursts, scallops | confident saturated-but-controlled pairs; bold color fields | chunky display, outline or offset shadow | bold contour, sticker or cut-paper energy | rich but organized; shapes interacting with the hero |
| Bold / street / youthful | strong blocks, diagonals, big type | hard-edged or heavy-rounded | high-contrast duo/trio | condensed, brush, stencil | poster print, halftone, linocut | texture, stamp or tape marks, deliberate roughness |
| Fresh / healthy | airy, vertical rhythm | leaf-like, curved | greens, pastels, sun tones | clean humanist, light script accent | botanical, clean | ingredient accents |
| Modest / dignified | ordered, symmetric | arch, geometric | deep tones, golds, creams | refined serif, restrained accents | restrained | geometric patterning, subtle |

Energetic registers are never answered with quiet, refined treatment; calm registers never get clutter. Match the energy, keep the craft.

### Style DNA (the unifier of all parts)
After setting the dials, fix a short set of shared parameters, and apply them to **every** element, including mark, illustrations, labels, **icons**, connector, and decoration:
- **Edge/corner language** (sharp, softly rounded, scalloped, torn, die-cut, arched, notched, ...)
- **Contour behavior** (none, thin, medium, bold; uniform or varied)
- **Fill logic** (flat, layered, overprint, cut-paper, halftone, ...)
- **Shadow logic** (none, hard offset, drop-shape, ...)
- **Texture** (grain, paper, print noise, none, ...)
- **Motif family** (the one recurring shape/pattern idea)
- **Color rule** (what each color is allowed to do)

State the Style DNA once in the Brief or Art direction; every zone block references it. A design looks unified when its parts *speak the same edge, line, and fill language*.

---

## 6. Direction proposals (when the owner can't pick a style)
Offer **2-3 distinct directions**, each as: **a name**, **the Design Idea in one line**, **mood in 3 words**, **how it differs from neighbors**, **one concrete visual cue**. Directions must differ on at least 3 dials, not be palette swaps. Let the owner pick, mix ("A tapi warnanya seperti B"), or ask for another. Record the choice as ✔ Confirmed. If the owner says "terserah", recommend one with a one-line reason (✚ Suggested).
