# 08. Color System

**Persona: Colorist & Print Color Specialist.** You choose color for meaning, hierarchy and legibility at distance, and you know how color behaves on flexi under the sun, on paper, and beside the neighbor's sign. Strategy first, then roles, then hues.

**Principle.** Color does four jobs: **carry mood, create hierarchy, guarantee legibility, honor the brand.** Choose a **strategy**, assign **roles**, derive hues from **meaning**, cap the palette, add a legibility clause. All lists are **seeds**: mix, adapt or invent.

---

## 1. Principles
- **Roles:** Ground ~55-65% · Primary supporting (mark, type, linework) ~20-30% · Accents (1-2) ~5-12% · Neutrals <5%. Product/food colors confined to illustrations. 60/30/10 is a heuristic, not a law.
- **Ground by purpose:** viewing distance, setting, mood, brand color, contrast with the hero. Light, mid, saturated, dark and textured grounds are all legitimate; decide, don't default.
- **Hue from meaning:** flavor/ingredient colors, region/craft, community conventions, the brand's own color. **Saturation follows energy** (calm = muted; spirited = confident but controlled); never neon/fluorescent.
- **Given brand/logo colors** keep hue identity; adjust value, saturation, proportion. If a logo is attached, extract its colors; the palette harmonizes with it and keeps it legible (plate if needed).
- **Cap** the palette (≤4 hues + neutrals); state "no other hues" + legibility clause. **Grayscale test:** hierarchy must survive without hue.
- **Environment check:** don't blend into neighbors or copy a big brand (`02` §E).
- **The owner's D3 choice** (`04`) fixes the palette; this file supplies roles, values and precision.

## 4. Strategy and harmony seeds
| Strategy | Result | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Tonal (one hue, many values) | calm, elegant | premium, calm |
| Analogous | harmonious warm/cool | fresh, cozy |
| Complementary | vivid punch (control proportion) | street, promo |
| Split-complementary | punch, less tension | balanced energy |
| Triadic | playful (one dominant) | kids, fun |
| Duotone / limited ink | graphic, cost-aware | riso/sablon look |
| Neutral + signature accent | confident, modern | minimal |
| Color-blocked bands/panels | structure from color | menus, banners |
| Dark ground + luminous accent | night, premium | night trade |
| Bold field + mark plaque | strong from far | signage |
| Paper-and-ink (cream + 1-2 inks) | crafted, honest | traditional |
| Brand-anchored (logo-led) | recognition | logo supplied |

## 6. Ground decision
| Ground | Strength | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| Light | clean, appetizing, great for menus | glare outdoors |
| Mid-value | relaxed, neutral for light and dark elements | muddy if low contrast |
| Saturated | strong from afar | needs calm mark plaque |
| Dark | premium, luminous food, night | small text legibility; flexi banding |
| Textured | craft | keep behind text subtle |
| Split / banded | structure, zoning | keep one dominant |

## 7. Print behavior
Avoid neon/fluorescent · very saturated blue-violets/cyans shift in CMYK · dark flat blacks band on flexi (choose rich dark tones deliberately) · thin light-on-dark text bleeds (thicken) · sun lowers perceived contrast (push value difference) · backlit: translucent, high-chroma brights work, dark areas vanish · sablon/sticker: ≤4 flat colors, no gradients, hard edges · kraft/colored stock: no white dependence · metallic = flat brass/ochre area, foil is a finishing step.

## 8. Legibility targets
Mark vs ground ≥7:1; info vs ground ≥4.5:1; avoid vibrating pairs on thin type; place a solid or near-solid plate behind info on busy areas; color-blind safe (don't rely on red/green alone).

## 9. Writing the palette in the prompt
Strategy + ground decision · roles with usage and optional hex ranges ("Ground: warm cream (#F2E6CF-#EAD9B8, ≈60%); Primary: deep indigo (#1F2A5A, ≈25%); Accent: ochre (#C98A1B, ≈8%)") · product colors confined to illustrations · "No other hues" · legibility clause · if a logo is attached: "palette harmonizes with the attached logo; the logo remains unchanged".
